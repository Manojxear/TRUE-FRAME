AI vs Real Content Detector

An intelligent content detection application designed to help users identify whether digital content is AI-generated or created by humans.

Features

- Image Detection: Analyze images to identify potential AI-generated content.
- Video Detection: Examine videos for signs of AI-generated or manipulated content.
- Text Detection: Analyze text to estimate whether it was written by AI or a human.
- Simple Interface: Easy-to-use experience for everyone.
- All-in-One Detection: Image, video, and text analysis in one application.

Purpose

Our goal is to make digital content verification easier and help users become more aware of AI-generated media.

Note: AI detection is not always 100% accurate. Results should be treated as estimates, not definitive proof.
